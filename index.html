What does smoosh.media do?⌄

Smoosh converts, compresses, and delivers your video, audio, and images. Prepare one file or put a whole workflow on autopilot, with output settings that fit the way you work.

Which formats can I use?⌄

Work with your everyday video, audio, and image formats, including MOV, MP4, WAV, MP3, PNG, JPEG, WebP, and AVIF. Choose a format yourself or use a preset for web, sharing, and storage.

Can I control the quality?⌄

Yes. Choose your output format, resolution, and quality, then save those settings as a preset. Keep more detail for final delivery or make a lighter version for a quick review.

Can I process a whole folder?⌄

Absolutely. Add a batch or connect a folder to a workflow. Smoosh applies your settings to each file and keeps the results together, ready for the next step.

How do automatic workflows work?⌄

Pick an input, choose your conversion and compression settings, and set a destination. New media is prepared and delivered as it arrives. Set it once, then let it run.
