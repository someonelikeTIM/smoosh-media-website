What do I get with the free scanner?⌄

A health report for your media library: file sizes and formats, the biggest optimization opportunities, and potential disk and cloud-storage savings. Download it for macOS or Windows 11.

Does a smaller file always mean a smaller cloud bill?⌄

With usage-based storage, reducing billable storage can lower charges directly. With Google One, iCloud+, and Dropbox, savings depend on moving to a smaller tier or delaying an upgrade. Staying on the same flat-fee plan gives you more headroom at the same price.

How are the storage savings calculated?⌄

The calculator retains each monthly batch through month 12, applies your chosen size reduction to media, and keeps other cloud files unchanged. It prices each monthly snapshot using the selected provider. The equations and source prices are shown with the results.

How is monthly usage counted?⌄

Source GB count each processing run before compression. Delivery GB count every copy sent to a destination. Each conversion uses at least one processing credit; longer conversions use more. All allowances reset monthly, including on yearly plans. Additional usage is agreed before processing.

How long are prepared files available?⌄

Prepared files are available for delivery for 48 hours. Completed deliveries stay in the destinations you choose.

How do I choose the right plan?⌄

Creator is for preparing media on demand, with batch processing and quality presets. Studio adds watched folders, automatic workflows, shared presets, and multi-destination delivery. Enterprise is priced around your business needs, media volume, and delivery requirements. The free scanner is available separately to help you understand your library first.
